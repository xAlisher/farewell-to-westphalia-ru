# Lessons — recurring translation error patterns

(appended by chapter closers; injected into every later translator prompt)

## chapter_00_front_matter

- **Naturalness slips persist even in formulaic front matter** (naturalness, 3 hits, all minor; no majors — the ch. 01/02 idiom-calque and deverbal-noun lessons are landing). Even boilerplate (copyright page, acknowledgements, category labels) needs the «сказал бы так русский автор?» reread: publishing-trade formulas have fixed Russian equivalents (печатное/электронное издание, оптовым покупателям, «он же» for aka) — reach for the native trade formula first, don't translate the English one.

## chapter_01_introduction

- **Glossary terms drift when they collide with neighbouring words** (terminology, 5 hits, 3 major). corruption-resistant / corruption resistance was rendered «защищён(ность) от коррупции» in 01-002/01-003/01-014 instead of the canonical «устойчив(ость) к коррупции» — apparently to avoid repeating «устойчивый» next to resilient. Rule: glossary terms are invariant, especially the book's four named base values (децентрализация, кооперация, устойчивость к коррупции, прозрачность); on collision with resilient, restructure the sentence («устойчивы — и к сбоям, и к коррупции»), never re-coin the term.
- **English idiom calques** (naturalness, 25 hits, 4 major). «взрыв инструментов» (explosion of tools), «на ближайшем горизонте» (on the immediate horizon). Rule: before submitting, reread each sentence asking «сказал бы так русский автор?»; replace literal image transfers with a native idiom («лавина инструментов», «не за горами») even at the cost of the source metaphor.
- **Deverbal-noun clusters / translationese syntax** (naturalness, same category). «участвует в устройстве процветания человека (и его провалов)», «их будущее принятие шло с как можно меньшим трением». Rule: unpack abstract noun chains into verbs with human subjects («стоит за тем, как люди процветают — или терпят крах», «чтобы их принимали как можно легче»); watch stacked genitives that create wrong attachments («провалов человека»).

## chapter_02_nation_states_are_obsolete_governance_technologies

- **Verb-government collisions and comic word-clashes** (naturalness, 43 hits, 2 major). «жизнь людей … улучшали хуже» (улучшать + хуже = оксюморон-стык), «сообща заниматься … изменением климата» (EN «issues» выпало, и «заниматься» стал напрямую управлять «изменением» — читается как «менять климат»). Rule: after eliding an EN hypernym (issues, matters, questions), re-check what the Russian verb now governs; reread each sentence aloud for adjacent-word clashes (улучшали хуже, повышали ниже) and restructure («решать вопросы климата», «с улучшением … справлялись хуже»).
- **Канцелярит creeps back at paragraph openings** (register, 2 hits but 1 major; echoes ch. 01 translationese pattern). «могут служить (и служат) тому, чтобы … осуществлять свой суверенитет в полной мере» — «служить тому, чтобы» plus banned «осуществлять» (styleguide §1). Rule: banned-verb list (осуществлять, являться, обеспечивать…) applies with extra force in a paragraph's first sentence — replace with a concrete verb («урезать суверенитет», «распоряжаться суверенитетом»).

## chapter_18_about_the_authors

- **Naturalness still dominates even in a two-paragraph bio page** (naturalness, 4 hits, all minor; digest kept no samples). Dense biographical sentences — stacked credentials, book titles, «came to X through Y» constructions — pull the translation back toward EN sentence shape. ## chapter_17_values_and_the_technology_stack

- **Broken verb/noun government inside long abstract sentences** (naturalness, 13 hits, 2 major). «открыть вам пути делать то…» — «путь» не присоединяет голый инфинитив; «Будущее у мира … возможно лишь такое, в котором» — подлежащее и сказуемое разорваны длинной вставкой и не согласуются. Rule: in sentences with a parenthetical insertion or an abstract head noun (путь, будущее, возможность), re-check the government chain after drafting — the noun must actually license what follows; if subject and predicate end up separated by a long insertion, recast as a conditional/if-clause («Если у … есть будущее, то лишь такое, где…») instead of preserving EN word order.
- **Mechanics minors still leak in threes** (mechanics, 3 hits, all minor; digest kept no samples). Rule: before submitting a chunk, run the mechanics micro-pass separately from the style reread — punctuation around «—» insertions, footnote-marker placement after closing punctuation, and list-item end punctuation are the usual suspects.
- **v2 pass: naturalness is still the residual leak, but shrinking** (naturalness, 3 hits, all minor; digest kept no samples; down from 13 in v1). Same class as the v1 majors — EN sentence shape surviving in long abstract sentences. Rule: the fixes hold, but the «сказал бы так русский автор?» reread plus the verb-government check stay mandatory on every chunk even in revision passes — minors persist precisely in sentences already reworked once.

## chapter_04_new_conceptual_foundations

- **Naturalness remains the top leak in essayistic/historical prose** (naturalness, 10 hits, all minor; digest kept no samples). Same class as ch. 01/02/17: EN sentence shape and idiom pull through in dense argumentative passages. Rule: the «сказал бы так русский автор?» reread plus the verb-government check are non-negotiable for every chunk, not just the ones that feel awkward — minors cluster precisely where the draft reads "fine enough".
- **Accuracy minors cluster in fact-dense narrative chunks** (accuracy, 7 hits, all minor; digest kept no samples). Chapter mixes history (dates, actors, org names: WANK, Hong Kong Blondes, WikiLeaks, Arab Spring) with quoted manifestos — small shifts of nuance/scope slip in when smoothing style. Rule: after the naturalness pass, do a separate EN↔RU alignment pass on chunks with names/dates/quotes, checking each claim's scope (who did what, claimed vs. proven, exact quoted wording) before accepting the smoother phrasing.
- **Glossary formulas are invariant even under stylistic pressure** (terminology, 2 hits but 1 major — the chapter's only major). «decentralised yet cooperative» drifted to «— и при этом кооперативными», dissolving the glossary-fixed «децентрализованный, но кооперативный» (glossary explicitly bans replacing «но»). Echoes ch. 01's term-drift lesson. Rule: the book's signature formulas must be pasted verbatim from the glossary, then the sentence restructured around them — never the reverse.
- **v2 pass: naturalness still the dominant leak, now the majority of all findings** (naturalness, 15 of 23 hits, all minor; digest kept no samples). Same class as v1's ch. 04 bullet and every other chapter: EN sentence shape surviving in essayistic/historical prose, clustering exactly where the draft reads "fine enough". Rule: unchanged and non-negotiable — the «сказал бы так русский автор?» reread plus the verb-government/collocation check on every chunk, including chunks already reworked in a prior pass.
- **v2 pass: accuracy + addition minors still cluster in the fact-dense narrative spans** (accuracy 4 + addition 3, all minor; digest kept no samples). The chapter's mix of dates, actors and quoted manifestos (WANK, Hong Kong Blondes, WikiLeaks, Arab Spring, Hughes/Johnson/Post quotes) keeps inviting small scope shifts and smuggled-in connectives when smoothing style. Rule: keep the separate EN↔RU alignment sweep after the style pass on chunks with names/dates/quotes — verify each claim's scope and that no explanatory glue was added that the EN does not license.

## chapter_03_post_state_governance

- **Calqued noun compounds around 'government/governance'** (naturalness, 20 hits, incl. the chapter's only major). «самые заметные формы человеческого правительства» for "the most visible forms of human government" — «правительство» in Russian is a concrete organ, it neither combines with «человеческий» nor forms «формы». Rule: when EN uses government/governance as an abstract mass noun, translate the concept, not the noun — «власть», «формы (само)организации», «управление» — and re-ask «сказал бы так русский автор?» specifically at government/governance phrases; this chapter is saturated with them.
- **Accuracy minors cluster in fact-dense case studies** (accuracy 5 + addition 3 + omission 2; digest kept no samples). Chapter is a chain of named cases (Cherán, Reedy Creek/Disney, The Villages, Hammocks HOA) full of dates, sums and legal specifics — smoothing the style shifts scope or drops/invents small qualifiers. Rule: after the style pass, run a separate EN↔RU alignment pass on case-study chunks — verify every number, name, date and hedged claim (alleged/perhaps/by some accounts) survived unchanged.
- **Mechanics micro-pass still needed per chunk** (mechanics, 4 hits, all minor; digest kept no samples; echoes ch. 17). Rule: keep the dedicated mechanics sweep — «—» punctuation, footnote-marker position after closing punctuation, list-item end punctuation — separate from the style reread.
- **[v2 pass] Attribute-transfer calques survive even after the government/governance lesson** (naturalness, 17 hits, incl. the pass's only major). «тайный бюллетень» for "secret ballots" — the EN adjective sticks to the wrong noun; по-русски тайным бывает голосование, не бюллетень. Rule: for every EN adjective+noun pair, check which noun the adjective belongs to in Russian usage («тайное голосование (бюллетенями)», not «тайный бюллетень») — same family as «человеческое правительство» above.
- **[v2 pass] Terminology drift now leaks in threes too** (terminology, 3 hits; alongside accuracy 4, omission 3, mechanics 3 — the same recurring clusters as v1). Rule: the per-chunk closing sweep is now four-fold — naturalness reread, EN↔RU alignment on facts/hedges, mechanics micro-pass, and a glossary/forbidden-variants grep — none of the four may be skipped even when the chunk «reads fine».

## chapter_12_how_blockchain_communities_will_collaborate

- **Nested-clause calques break Russian syntax in abstract argumentation** (naturalness, 20 hits, 2 major). «Нельзя прояснить то, о чём вы уже не помните, чтó именно требовало прояснения» — the EN relative-clause nesting misfires and the sentence stops parsing; «стратегий обращения с несотрудничающими сообществами» — artificial participle-calque plus genitive chain. Rule: when the EN sentence embeds a clause inside a clause (lost track of what it is that…, dealing with X-ing Y), do not transplant the frame — recast as a conditional or a «которые …»-clause («если вы уже не помните, что именно…», «сообществами, которые отказываются сотрудничать») and rerun the «сказал бы так русский автор?» check on every nested construction.
- **Glossary terms drift under stylistic pressure — even deliberate euphemisms** (terminology, 5 hits, incl. the 3-judge-unanimous major). "becoming kinetic" became «горячую фазу» — a forbidden variant that erases the military euphemism the authors use on purpose and that ch. 13 builds on; «кросс-чейн» appeared for the fixed «кроссчейн». Rule: paste glossary renderings verbatim («кинетический», «кроссчейн-…») and restructure the sentence around them; a term feeling stylistically odd in RU is often the point (сохранять как эвфемизм) — check forbidden_variants before "improving" it.
- **Accuracy/addition minors cluster where style is smoothed** (accuracy 4 + addition 4 + omission 1; mechanics 3; digest kept no samples; echoes ch. 03/04/09). Rule: keep the two separate sweeps per chunk — EN↔RU alignment on scope/hedges/names after the style pass, and the mechanics micro-pass («—» punctuation, footnote-marker position, list-item end punctuation) before submitting.

## chapter_10_rethinking_sovereignty

- **Naturalness dominates again in abstract political-theory prose** (naturalness, 26 hits, 2 major). Majors were classic calques: anacoluthon «Следующий шаг в понимании суверенитета — различим…» (nominal opener broken by a 1st-person verb — after a «X — …» opener the continuation must be an infinitive or the frame recast as «Сделаем следующий шаг: …») and «идея … не бессвязна» for "not incoherent" (по-русски «бессвязной» бывает речь, не концепция — «не лишена смысла», «не так уж несостоятельна»). Rule: same «сказал бы так русский автор?» reread on every chunk, with extra attention to (a) nominal sentence openers whose continuation must agree grammatically and (b) EN evaluative adjectives about concepts (incoherent, compelling, robust) — translate the judgement, not the adjective.
- **Abbreviation false friends are glossary violations, not style choices** (terminology, 3 hits, 2 major on the same span). NGO drifted to «НКО» in 10-021 even though 10-017 in the same section correctly introduced «неправительственная организация (НПО)» — a forbidden variant with the wrong referent (Russian legal category). Rule: once a term with a fixed abbreviation is introduced in a chapter, grep the remaining chunks for the rival abbreviation before submitting; forbidden_variants that look like near-synonyms (НКО/НПО) are exactly where drift happens.
- **Accuracy minors keep clustering where style is smoothed** (accuracy 3 + addition 2; echoes ch. 03/04/09/12). Chapter mixes theory with fact-dense spans (Monaco/Nauru/Tuvalu/Vatican figures, Castronova's Ultima Online economics, SEZ examples). Rule: keep the separate EN↔RU alignment sweep on chunks with numbers, names and quoted material after the naturalness pass — verify scope, hedges and figures survived unchanged.

## chapter_07_why_centralisation_is_the_problem

- **Verb-government calques and collocation breaks dominate again** (naturalness, 27 hits, 2 major). Majors were textbook cases: «применяется борьба с отмыванием денег» (борьбу ведут, меры применяют — the collocation broke because 'policies' silently dropped out) and «лоббировать против таких мер» (English government of *lobby against*; по-русски лоббируют что-то, а рычагов может «не быть», но «нечем лоббировать» не говорят). Rule: the «сказал бы так русский автор?» reread with an explicit verb-government/collocation check on every verb+noun pair; and when a noun like *policies/measures* feels redundant in RU, keep its function — dropping it is what breaks the collocation.
- **Direction-of-action ambiguity is an accuracy trap, not a style nit** (accuracy 6 + omission 3 + addition 2, 1 major). "dollar they spend on financial crimes" became «потраченный на финансовые преступления» — reads as funding crime, not fighting it. Rule: during the EN↔RU alignment sweep, flag every «на + деятельность» phrase and every spend/fight/target construction — make the direction of action explicit («на борьбу с…», «против…») even when EN leaves it implicit.
- **Canonical titles from proper-nouns.json are verbatim, like glossary terms** (terminology, 4 hits, 2 major on one span, 2 judges independently). *Where Is the Wealth of Nations?* drifted to «Где богатство наций?» although the canonical form «Где богатство народов?» deliberately echoes Смита «Богатство народов». Rule: before submitting a chunk with any book/report title, look it up in proper-nouns.json and paste the fixed form — a title that alludes to another canonical title must keep the allusion, so «improving» it word-by-word is always wrong.
